Listen guide

Each data point becomes one note, played left to right in time (it also pans from your left ear to your right).

You hearIt means
Higher pitchA higher value, compared with the rest of this series (notes snap to a pentatonic scale over two octaves, from the series minimum to its maximum).
Brighter toneAlso a higher value: the filter opens as the value rises.
Faster wobbleA big jump from the previous point.
Left → rightEarliest → latest date.
Soft dull tick, almost silentNo data for that date (for example, a cloudy satellite pass). We don't make up a value. A single missing point between two good ones is bridged smoothly and marked “interpolated” in the chart and table.

Voices: Ocean Symphony uses warm sine waves over a deep drone; Forest Voices uses an airy pad; Earth's Pulse uses a firmer, minor-key tone.

Pitch is relative to each series. Compare places by reading the numbers in “Understand”, not by ear alone.
